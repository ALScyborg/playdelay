FAQ

Questions people ask before the game

Short answers are here. The long version of lining up a broadcast is the sync guide. Stations and confirmed voices are on the team radio page.

Is PlayDelay free?

You can play now without paying. The paywall is not enforced, and the home page still describes this stretch as free while the product is being tested. The pricing page lists planned credits — $2 for one game, or $20 for 14 games — and the Stripe checkout links on that page are in test mode. Buying is optional. Credits are not required to open the player today. When a paywall is actually turned on, that page is where the change will show.

What devices does it run on?

PlayDelay is a website. There is nothing to install from an app store. Open it in the browser on a phone, a tablet, or a computer, then sign in. An account is an email address and a password of at least six characters. The player is laid out for a phone in one hand: Play, mute, the presets, and the delay buttons are large on purpose. On a computer keyboard, Space plays or pauses, M mutes, and the up and down arrows change the delay by one second.

The delay itself needs the browser’s audio engine. When a station blocks that path, the player still plays live radio and tells you the delay control is unavailable. Headphones or a small speaker are enough. The television stays muted so you are not hearing two broadcasts.

Why is the radio ahead of the TV?

They are different productions of the same game. The flagship radio call is mixed at the stadium and sent to the station with very little waiting. The television picture goes through the network truck, encoding, and then another buffer in your cable box, satellite receiver, or streaming app. YouTube TV and Hulu + Live TV hold extra video so the picture does not stall, which is why those apps are usually further behind a cable box in the same house. The announcer is not talking early. The picture is late. Holding the radio is how you put them back together. The sync guide walks through that gap in more detail.

How do I pick a delay?

Mute the television. Play the station. Wait for one sharp sound you can also see: a snap, a whistle, a kick, or the horn. If you hear it first, add delay. If you see it first, move toward Live. The presets are Live, 5, 8, 10, and 15 seconds. Under those, the buttons step by 1, 5, or 10 seconds. The longest delay is 120 seconds.

For a cable or satellite box, start at 8 seconds and nudge by one. For YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV, or another streaming app, start at 15 seconds and add time if the call is still early after the picture has settled. An antenna is often closer to Live. Use the number that matches the screen in front of you, not the number that worked on a different app last week. When you press Play with a delay already set, the player stays quiet for about that many seconds while it fills the buffer. That pause is labeled “Building delay…”. It is not a stalled stream.

Which teams are included?

The full Big 12 is in the player: Arizona, Arizona State, Baylor, BYU, Cincinnati, Colorado, Houston, Iowa State, Kansas, Kansas State, Oklahoma State, TCU, Texas Tech, UCF, Utah, and West Virginia. The Big Ten, the ACC, and the SEC are in the player too, along with Notre Dame. Ole Miss is the Power 4 football school that is not listed.

Football schedules use the 2026 season. Men’s basketball uses the current ESPN season. Times show in your timezone. Radio is live for every team on that list except Kansas State. Kansas State’s games still appear on the schedule; the stream is marked coming soon. Ole Miss is omitted because its 2026 Oxford home station, WOXF 105.1, does not publish a stream the player can open. The station each team uses, and the play-by-play voice when a 2026 source named one, is on the team radio page. Your favorites, once you save them, sort to the top of the picker. The default favorites shown before you edit them are BYU, Utah, Arizona State, USC, Miami, and Arizona.

How do I request a team?

Email support@playdelay.app. Name the school and the station you actually listen to, including the city and frequency if you know them. There is no request button in the player. The same address is the right place if a station in the app is wrong or a stream that used to play has gone silent.

What does PlayDelay do with my information?

Creating an account stores your email address. Favorite teams you choose are saved with that account. The site records product usage — which team you select, play and stop, schedule views, and about how long you listen — so the rough edges can be found. Messages you type into the Help chat are support messages. The last team, the last sport (football or men’s basketball), and the delay you settled on stay in the browser as well.

PlayDelay does not sell your email list. Advertising, when it runs, is on pages that explain the product. The sign-in screen, the player, and the pricing screen do not load the ad script. Processors that help run the site, including the account system, receive only what that job needs. The full account is the privacy policy. To delete an account or ask what is stored, email support@playdelay.app or use the Help chat. Signing out does not erase on-device preferences; clearing the site’s data in the browser does.
