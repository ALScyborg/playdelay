Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-19. PlayDelay is operated by PlayDelay LLC (Arizona).

What we collect

  • Account: email address when you sign up (Supabase Auth), plus optional favorite teams you choose.
  • Usage: team selects, play/stop, schedule views, and listen duration so we can improve the product (stored in Supabase).
  • Support: messages you send through the on-site Help chat.
  • Device preferences: last team, delay, mute, and similar settings in your browser localStorage.

How we use it

We use this data to run PlayDelay (sign-in, sync radio to TV, show schedules), fix bugs, understand which teams people use, and answer support requests. We do not sell your email list.

Advertising

PlayDelay may show third-party ads (including Google AdSense) to keep the product free. Ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show relevant ads. See our Advertising page and Google’s advertising policies. You can adjust ad personalization in your Google account settings.

Sharing

We use processors that help run the service (for example Supabase for auth/database, Google for ads when enabled, and hosting for the static site). They only get what they need to provide that service.

Retention & your choices

You can sign out anytime. To delete your account or ask about the data we hold, email support@playdelay.app or use the Help chat on the site. Local preferences stay on your device until you clear site data.

Children

PlayDelay is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
