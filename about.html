About

Keep the picture. Hear the call you wanted.

PlayDelay was built by Brad Smith. He has ALS and is a Neuralink trial participant. The project is a practical answer to a game-night problem he was not willing to give up: the television shows the game, and the radio station you grew up with calls it, and those two things do not arrive at the same time.

Network television is a national feed. It is well shot, and it is not the voice a lot of fans actually want in the room. The local radio broadcast — the flagship station, the play-by-play that has said the school’s name for years — is usually ahead of that picture, because the video path is buffered and the radio path is not. Muting the TV and turning on the radio leaves you with a call that describes a play still coming. PlayDelay holds that radio stream, for up to two minutes, until the sound and the picture land together.

The player is shaped around that use, not around a dense remote control. Play, mute, and the delay buttons are large so they can be used with one hand. A delay you already chose has to stay available when the stream reconnects, because hunting through a menu mid-drive is how a game gets abandoned. Favorites sit at the top of the team list so the station you came for is the first tap, not the last.

The teams in the app are the ones with a flagship stream wired up: the full Big 12, Nebraska and USC, Miami, LSU, and Notre Dame. Football and men’s basketball schedules are pulled from ESPN and shown in your local time. Kansas State’s schedule is included; its radio stream is not connected yet. The station list, and the voices we could confirm, is on the team radio page. How to choose a delay for cable versus a streaming app such as YouTube TV or Hulu is the sync guide.

PlayDelay is operated by PlayDelay LLC in Arizona. You can use it now without paying. Planned game credits are listed on the pricing page, and that paywall is not enforced. An account is an email and a password. What the account stores, and what the ads on the reading pages are allowed to do, is spelled out in the privacy policy. The sign-in screen, the player, and pricing do not load advertising.

If a stream is silent, a station name is wrong, or you want a school that is not here yet, email support@playdelay.app. Say which team, and which station you listen to. That note is how the list grows.
